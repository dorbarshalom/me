Why not no-code

A production-grade product, not a disposable prototype

No-code platforms are great for quick proof-of-concepts. But when it's time to scale, they hit a wall: platform lock-in, rigid template boundaries, and failure to pass serious security or compliance checks. When I build for you, you own 100% of the code, on an architecture engineered to grow from day one, customized without limits to your needs.

About me

20 years shaping products — from first customer insight to production

I'm Dor Bar Shalom, an end-to-end AI product builder. I started as a designer and art director, evolved into UX architecture and front-end engineering, and spent the last decade leading product organizations — as Head of Product at WiseStamp and vCita, Director of Product Integrations at D-ID, and VP Product at Storydoc.

I live in data: Mixpanel, conversion funnels, session replays — but just as actively on the ground talking with users, B2B and B2C alike, to understand what people truly need rather than just what the dashboard displays.

Over the past year, I've redefined how I build: crafting production-grade products solo with an AI-native stack covering discovery, analysis, design, and deployment. That's how hiFred and Maintor came to life — both live in production today.
